### A25 - Fault-code numeric base is not explicit

DOCUMENTED: P p9 gives a simultaneous battery-undervoltage / inverter-overload example as `0x204: 01`, `0x205: 14`, `0x206: 00`, `0x207: 00`. The addresses are explicitly hexadecimal, while the fault-code values in that example have no explicit radix prefix. Manuals W pp50-52 / S pp53-54 label codes using bracketed digit strings such as 01, 14 and 20. The intended numeric representation of a received word versus the displayed two-digit fault label is not explicitly stated. UNVERIFIED: do not assume hexadecimal 14 is the same code as the manual label 14, or apply BCD conversion without confirmation. Raw fault words are directly supported; exact value-to-label decoding requires resolution of this example's notation. A25 supplements the missing bitmap definitions in A21.

## Critical clarification requests

1. Provide the exact hardware revision, installed main/slave firmware versions and the model-specific Modbus register applicability list. Does installed firmware actually implement V2.04, including 024C-0255 and F04D-F05D?
2. Confirm which physical connector hosts monitoring Modbus at 9600,N,8,1, whether a logger can coexist, and read-only access/password requirements. What are the official timing, timeout and safe polling limits?
3. Resolve grid active-power units/signs (A01), BMS current encoding (A04), battery-voltage settings including schedules (A06), range-column conventions (A07/A22), and F04C/F048 energy semantics (A19/A20).
4. Resolve E003 permission, E21A behavior, output/PV priority combinations and wiring-mode mapping. Supply SPI-specific current/power limits and defaults rather than family values.
5. Confirm CT measurement location/polarity, Home Load versus UPS/secondary-load accounting, and generator input telemetry. Supply the bit definitions for 020B, 0200-0203 and BMS flags; verify history depth and snapshot layout.
6. Resolve the two manuals' PV maximum voltage/MPPT range and identify which manual matches the actual device. This documentation audit cannot resolve a hardware-rating discrepancy.

All six requests remain UNVERIFIED. They do not authorize hardware access, writes, firmware changes or Home Assistant configuration. Stop for review after SRNE-01B.
